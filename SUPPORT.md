# Support

This release covers Minecraft 26 on the x64 operating system named in the
repository README. Fabric and NeoForge support covers the listed Minecraft
26.1–26.2 configurations. Individual mods and modpacks have not been tested.

Minecraft 26.3 Snapshot 10 is supported for vanilla single-player. Its
multiplayer behavior is expected but unverified, and its mod loaders are not
supported yet.

Use the OS-specific installer and correct MultiMC or OLauncher executable.
Report problems through this repository's Issues tab, including exact game,
loader, launcher and installer versions. Do not include credentials or tokens.

No universal compatibility, performance, security, or arbitrary-mod guarantee
is made. This is a community port for an unsupported operating system.
